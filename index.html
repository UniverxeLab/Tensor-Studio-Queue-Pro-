Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this extension send my prompts, images, or Tensor.art cookies to remote servers?

No. Everything runs 100% inside your own Chrome browser using Manifest V3 local storage (chrome.storage.local) and Chrome's native downloads API. We operate zero remote scraping servers and never see your prompts, Tensor.art account, or generated media. The only external request is optional Pro license validation directly against api.gumroad.com if you enter a key.

How is billing handled and who processes Pro payments?

Paid licenses ($4.90/mo Starter Pro and $29 Early-Bird Lifetime) are sold directly by UniverxeLab through Gumroad as Merchant of Record, not by Google or Chrome Web Store. Gumroad handles cards/PayPal, regional VAT/sales tax compliance, receipts, and instant email license key delivery.

What if Tensor.art updates its Create modal layout and selectors stop clicking?

Open the Setup & Pro tab and check Selector Health. Any affected box marks Missing and you can click Pick next to that control, then click the corresponding field inside Tensor.art to save a permanent local override in 5 seconds.

Why do you recommend re-scanning before exporting old batches to ZIP?

Tensor.art serves generated workspace outputs through expiring signed Cloudflare R2 URLs (valid for 1 hour). Clicking Scan Page inside Gallery automatically refreshes the latest download links for all visible creations without duplicating cards.

Is this an official Tensor.art product?

No. Studio Queue & Bulk Downloader for Tensor.art is an independent client-side productivity utility developed by UniverxeLab to help creators organize prompts and archives inside their own browser session. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tensor.art.
